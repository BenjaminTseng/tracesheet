# Executive Engineering Hiring Rubric (REQ-ENG-402)

## Cloud Economics Ownership (REQ-ENG-402-FINOPS)
Score 5/5 (Distinguished): Demonstrated ownership of >$20M cloud budget with >=25% unit-economic efficiency improvement without SLA degradation.

## Direct FTE Organization Scope (REQ-ENG-402-ORG-SCOPE)
Minimum baseline requires 60+ direct permanent FTE engineering reports; dotted-line systems integrator contractors are excluded from permanent leadership span calculations.
