# Federal Subject-Matter Jurisdiction Authorities

## [AUTH:28-USC-1332-A] 28 U.S.C. § 1332(a) — Diversity of Citizenship; Amount in Controversy
- **Title:** 28 U.S.C. § 1332(a)
- **URL:** https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28/1332
- **Verbatim Rule:** The district courts shall have original jurisdiction of all civil actions where the matter in controversy exceeds the sum or value of $75,000, exclusive of interest and costs, and is between citizens of different States.

## [AUTH:STRAWBRIDGE-1806] Strawbridge v. Curtiss, 7 U.S. (3 Cranch) 267 (1806)
- **Title:** Strawbridge v. Curtiss, 7 U.S. (3 Cranch) 267 (1806)
- **URL:** https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/7/267/
- **Verbatim Holding:** Complete diversity requires that no plaintiff be a citizen of the same state as any defendant.

## [AUTH:HERTZ-2010-NERVE-CENTER] Hertz Corp. v. Friend, 559 U.S. 77 (2010)
- **Title:** Hertz Corp. v. Friend, 559 U.S. 77 (2010)
- **URL:** https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/559/77/
- **Verbatim Holding:** 'Principal place of business' is best read as referring to the place where a corporation's officers direct, control, and coordinate the corporation's activities — the 'nerve center.'

## [AUTH:JOHNSON-2013-3CIR] Johnson v. SmithKline Beecham Corp., 724 F.3d 337 (3d Cir. 2013)
- **Title:** Johnson v. SmithKline Beecham Corp., 724 F.3d 337 (3d Cir. 2013)
- **URL:** https://case-law.vlex.com/vid/johnson-v-smithkline-beecham-88921402
- **Verbatim Holding:** When a holding or operating entity conducts its board governance and treasury approvals in Wilmington, Delaware is its nerve center regardless of remote engineering headcount.
